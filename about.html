Our Story

25 years of care, one patient at a time

MediCare Hospital opened its doors in 2001 with a single mission: deliver hospital-grade medicine with the warmth of a family doctor. What started as a 40-bed community clinic has grown into a 300-bed multi-specialty hospital serving over 40,000 patients.

We've stayed true to our founding belief — that good healthcare starts with listening. Every department, from emergency to rehabilitation, is built around clear communication and measurable outcomes.

What Drives Us

Mission & Vision

Our Mission
