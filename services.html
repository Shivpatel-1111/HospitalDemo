Emergency & Trauma Care

Our emergency department is staffed round the clock with trauma specialists, a dedicated resuscitation bay, and rapid ambulance dispatch.

  • Average ER response under 8 minutes
  • On-call trauma surgeons
  • GPS-tracked ambulance fleet
Precision Diagnostics

Imaging & Laboratory

On-site MRI, CT, X-ray, and ultrasound paired with a full-service pathology lab means most results are ready the same day.

  • Same-day report turnaround
  • Digital reports via patient portal
  • Home sample collection available
Advanced Surgical Care

Surgery & Operating Theatres

Six modern operating theatres equipped for minimally invasive and robotic-assisted procedures across specialties.

  • Minimally invasive & laparoscopic surgery
  • Dedicated post-op recovery suites
  • Certified Green OT standards

In-House Pharmacy

Our 24-hour pharmacy fills prescriptions on-site and offers home delivery for discharged and outpatient patients.

  • 24-hour dispensing counter
  • Same-day home delivery
  • Insurance claim assistance
